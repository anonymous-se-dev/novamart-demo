Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how NovaMart Corporation ("NovaMart", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies across our websites and apps, and how you can manage your preferences.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. First-party cookies are set by us; third-party cookies are set by our partners.

Managing your preferences

Your choices are captured and honoured by our Consent Management Platform, powered by Securiti. You can change them anytime by clicking , or the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link in our footer. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be switched off.

Consent management

NovaMart uses the Securiti Cookie Consent Management platform to record and honour your choices, and implements Google Consent Mode v2 so tags loaded through Google Tag Manager respect your selection before setting cookies. Your consent record includes the categories you allowed and a timestamp.
